# Sales Desk: 3-minute demo video (Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon)

**The job:** judges and X viewers, watching cold. One idea: *you pay Meta for chats, and Sales Desk shows which ad made money.* Answering every chat is how it gets there.
**Belief before:** "AI chatbots for WhatsApp are everywhere." **Belief after:** "This one ties naira in the bank back to the ad, and uses Meta's own models for every step."
**Name and benefit, said twice:** at the start and at the end.
**Narration:** about 290 words, about 2 min 40 s at 110 words a minute. Long waits for Muse are cut out in the edit.

Recorded by `video/record.py`. It drives the real app at the speed of the real models, then trims the silences.
