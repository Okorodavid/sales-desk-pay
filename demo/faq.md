## How do I pay?
By PayPal. Once you agree the price, Folake approves the order and the robot sends you a secure PayPal link. You can pay with your PayPal balance or a card. The order is confirmed the moment PayPal tells us the money arrived.

## Can I return fabric?
Uncut fabric can be returned within 14 days if it is unused. Cut fabric cannot be returned.

## Do you sell less than 6 yards?
Yes. Everything sold by the yard can be bought from 2 yards.

## Can I see the fabric before paying?
Yes. You can come to the shop on Rye Lane, Peckham, Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm.

## Do you sew?
No. We sell fabric only, but we can recommend tailors in south London.
