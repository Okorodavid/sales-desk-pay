## How do I pay?
Bank transfer to the account the shop sends you. Send the receipt or a screenshot in the chat, and Nkem confirms once the money lands.

## Can I return fabric?
Uncut fabric can be returned within 3 days if it is unused. Cut fabric cannot be returned.

## Do you sell less than 6 yards?
Yes. Everything sold by the yard can be bought from 2 yards.

## Can I see the fabric before paying?
Yes. You can come to the shop at Ogui Road, Enugu, Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm.

## Do you sew?
No. We sell fabric only, but we can recommend tailors in Enugu.
